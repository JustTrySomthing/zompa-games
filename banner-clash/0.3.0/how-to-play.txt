Win three banners to win the match.

The gold flag marks the current battle. Tap a card, choose its target, check the strength preview, then confirm. Each player takes five open turns per battle. Play a character, cast a spell, move, swap one card or pass.

Characters stay where you deploy them. A character at the next site can give you a head start. Tap a site to inspect its occupants.

After the open turns, each player secretly sends one character from hand or holds. Only its printed strength counts. Both choices are revealed together.

Higher strength at the flag wins one banner. If tied, compare combined strength at the other two sites. If still tied, both players earn a banner. Reaching three together is a draw.

Characters at the scored site retire and Rally bonuses expire. The flag travels Bridge, Watchtower, Grove, Watchtower, Bridge until someone wins.
