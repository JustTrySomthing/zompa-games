FORTRESS: THE BADLANDS — HOW TO PLAY

LOOK AT YOUR PIECES
Tap a piece to read its large card near the top of the screen: picture, name, kingdom, HP, MOVE, HIT. Red trim belongs to Player 1 (bottom); blue to Player 2 (top). Only injured units show a small health bar on the board. Full health is visible on the card.

YOUR TURN
Tap one of your pieces. Bright GREEN hexagons with center dots are open destinations; RED crossed swords on an enemy means you can land there and fight. Tap a destination and check it, then press MOVE HERE or ATTACK NOW. CANCEL changes your target. Each turn is one movement or attack.

ENEMY TURN
Watch the opponent's piece move across the board. If it attacks, the battle scene begins after the movement, so you can see what happened.

D8 BATTLES
Both sides roll a D8. The higher roll wins that exchange, deals that character's fixed HIT damage, and reduces the opponent's HP. A tie causes no damage. Watch each roll, successful strike, and health decrease. Exchanges continue until one piece falls; the surviving winner stays on its square (or takes the defeated enemy's square) and automatically heals 1–8 HP up to its maximum. The result screen stays open until you press CONTINUE.

GOAL
Destroy the enemy Fortress. Each kingdom has fourteen soldiers; Player 1 starts at the bottom and Player 2 (or CPU) at the top. The Badlands between them are neutral. No territory claiming.

HEALTH
Damage carries over. After defeating a soldier, the surviving winner receives 1–8 HP of healing (not above maximum). After each battle, tap ROLL D8 TO HEAL to reveal the reward, then CONTINUE. Healing is already determined during combat so both online devices agree.

FORTRESS
The Fortress starts with 35 HP. It never moves or attacks proactively. When directly attacked, its defenders roll a D8, dealing 5 damage on each successful exchange. Destroying the opposing Fortress wins the game.

FINAL SIEGE
If both armies are eliminated without a Fortress being destroyed, each surviving Fortress launches trebuchet volleys simultaneously. Both roll their own D8. Rolls 4–8 hit for 7 damage; rolls 1–3 miss. Trebuchet volleys are shown at a readable pace. BOTH may hit in one round, even if their rolls match. If both Fortresses fall together, the ending is MUTUALLY ASSURED DESTRUCTION.

MODES
Single Player: you are Player 1 against the CPU. Pass & Play: share one device. Online 2-Player: invite a friend through Zompa; the app handles the match and turn delivery. The Zompa toolbar provides Back, Help, Audio, and Pause.

VISUALS
All movement rings and playing pieces are centered on the same painted square. Battle portraits show only their hexagonal badges, and the D8 spins in three dimensions before landing on the final result.

BATTLE DISPLAY
Red and Blue identify kingdoms. Larger fighter portraits, paired dice and health bars show the action. Red damage bubbles (-5, etc.) appear on the unit that lost the roll. A clear kingdom and character victory announcement replaces verbose status text.

VISUAL FEEDBACK
The golden glow identifies the selected soldier; the brighter white-gold destination indicates the square that MOVE HERE or ATTACK NOW will confirm. Battles show damage flashes and a brief pause on the final hit. A fallen Fortress gets its own end-of-match animation. These effects never change combat rolls, movement or saved turns.

BATTLE PRESENTATION
Red soldiers roll a red D8; Blue soldiers roll a blue D8. After the final blow, a large angled banner identifies the winning kingdom and soldier. Tap ROLL FOR +1 TO +8 HP to reveal the winner's bonus; the computer reveals its own reward automatically. The bonus is calculated with combat before the dialog, so it is the same on all devices.

LAYOUT
The character inspector stays in its dedicated top area and does not cover the board. The board keeps its proportions while filling the usable display more tightly.

APPROVED FORTRESS VISUAL REDESIGN
Your board still has 58 playable spaces and the same movement rules, now rendered as contiguous six-sided stone slabs. Select a piece, inspect its card above the board and confirm MOVE HERE or ATTACK NOW at the bottom. During battle, red and blue combat dice match their kingdoms. After victory, the winning side rolls for a 1–8 HP bonus, capped by the unit's maximum HP. The last two combat dice disappear when the center reward die starts; the result announces HEALTH BONUS +N without extra HP text. The CPU reveals its reward automatically. All bonuses are determined by the game's shared rules; tapping the animation never changes the roll.

VERSION 0.4.8 VISUAL IMPROVEMENTS
The Red and Blue Fortresses are visible on the first and last row again. The stone hexes have no dark center seam. During battle, a damage value rises from the wounded unit's health bar toward its badge and then fades. Victory and health-bonus banners have raised gilded borders. All visual changes leave game rules and D8 outcomes unchanged.
