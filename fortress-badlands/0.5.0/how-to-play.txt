FORTRESS: THE BADLANDS — HOW TO PLAY (v0.5.0)

THE NEW BOARD
The hourglass arena is an 11-row layout of 56 joined hexagons, with row sizes 3,4,5,6,7,6,7,6,5,4,3. The six hexes around a stone are its adjacent spaces. The weathered floor and thin grout separate movement locations without gaps.

YOUR ARMY
Red (Player 1) begins at the bottom. Blue (Player 2/CPU) starts at the top. Each has 15 movable troops plus the stationary two-tower Fortress in the CENTER hex of the back row.

Four Soldiers: 22 HP, MOVE 2, HIT 5.
Four Ogres: 28 HP, MOVE 1, HIT 6.
Three Barbarians: 19 HP, MOVE 2, HIT 6.
Two Assassins: 14 HP, MOVE 3, HIT 6.
Two Wizards: 16 HP, MOVE 1, HIT 8.

The rear rank has Soldier, Fortress, Soldier. In front are Wizard, Ogre, Ogre, Wizard. Next are Ogre, Assassin, Barbarian, Assassin, Ogre. The forward rank has an empty end hex, then Soldier, Barbarian, Barbarian, Soldier, then an empty end hex.

TAKING A TURN
Tap one of your troops to inspect its HP, MOVE and HIT. Choose any highlighted clear hex within that troop's maximum move range, then press MOVE HERE. To attack, select a highlighted enemy-occupied hex and press ATTACK NOW. Each turn takes one move or attack. Troops cannot pass through occupied hexes or missing edge cells. You can travel through connected hexes in six directions and turn during a move.

D8 BATTLE
Both units roll a D8. The higher die attacks and deals its class's HIT value; equal rolls do no damage. Exchanges continue until a troop falls. If the attacker wins, it moves into the defeated troop's hex. The survivor's winning bonus is another D8, giving 1–8 HP without exceeding its maximum. The button reveals the deterministic reward, so both players' matches agree. The losing side's bonus is revealed automatically when necessary. Red dice represent Red and blue dice represent Blue.

VICTORY AND FORTRESSES
Destroy the enemy Fortress to win. A Fortress has 35 HP, cannot move or attack proactively, and deals five HP damage whenever its defensive D8 wins a battle exchange. If both armies are eliminated before either Fortress falls, both use trebuchets in Final Siege: a D8 of 4–8 deals 7 HP to the enemy fortress, simultaneously. Both fortresses may fall at once.

MODES
Single Player: Red versus Blue CPU. Pass & Play: two players share a device. Online 2-Player: Zompa manages invitations, turn syncing, and match delivery. Back, Help, Sound and Pause are host toolbar controls.

UPDATING FROM OLD BOARD VERSIONS
This redesign changes the starting armies and battlefield geometry. The new v6 save format cannot resume local matches from the earlier 14-troop board. Start a new game. Previously started online matches remain on their own pinned version.

VISUAL INDICATORS
The illuminated unit is selected; gold outlines indicate possible moves, and red crossed swords indicate an attack destination. Injured units show a small health bar. Battle damage rises near the HP bar, and the arcade victory banner precedes the ROLL / CONTINUE control.
