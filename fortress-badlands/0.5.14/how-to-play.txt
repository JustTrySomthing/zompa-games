FORTRESS: THE BADLANDS — HOW TO PLAY (v0.5.14)

THE NEW BOARD
The hourglass arena is an 11-row layout of 56 joined hexagons, with row sizes 3,4,5,6,7,6,7,6,5,4,3. The six hexes around a stone are its adjacent spaces. The weathered floor and thin grout separate movement locations without gaps.

YOUR ARMY
Red (Player 1) begins at the bottom. Blue (Player 2/CPU) starts at the top. Each has 15 movable troops plus the stationary two-tower Fortress in the CENTER hex of the back row.

Four Soldiers: 22 Health, MOVE 1 forward, HIT 5.
Two Ogres: 28 Health, MOVE 1, HIT 10.
Three Barbarians: 32 Health, MOVE 2, HIT 8.
Two Assassins: 24 Health, MOVE LINE, HIT 8.
Two Wizards: 24 Health, TELEPORT 2 hexes (over pieces), HIT 8.
Two Archers: 20 Health, MOVE 4 in a straight line over troops, HIT 5.

The rear rank has Barbarian, Fortress, Barbarian. In front are Wizard, Archer, Archer, Wizard. Next are Ogre, Assassin, Barbarian, Assassin, Ogre. The forward rank has an empty end hex, then four Soldiers and an empty hex on the other flank.

TAKING A TURN
Tap one of your troops to inspect its Health, MOVE and HIT. Choose any highlighted clear hex within that troop's maximum move range, then press MOVE HERE. To attack, select a highlighted enemy-occupied hex and press ATTACK NOW. Each turn takes one move or attack. Ordinary moves stop at occupied hexes. Missing edge cells block all movement. Soldiers can move or attack only one hex forward, toward the enemy Fortress. Choose either adjacent hex in the next row toward the enemy. Ogres move one hex in any direction. Wizards teleport directly to a destination within two hexes, crossing other pieces (but not landing on allies). The Assassin moves any distance in one of the six straight hex directions. It can stop on an empty hex or land on the first enemy in that line to attack. Any occupied hex ends the line. Barbarians may turn while following legal connected hexes. Archers move or attack along a straight line of up to four hexes. They can jump over friendly or enemy troops, but they cannot jump over a Fortress or land on an ally.

D8 BATTLE
Press the green ROLL button before each exchange. When an exchange first leaves the attacker alive below half maximum Health, the attacker gets one chance to RETREAT. Press RETREAT to return to the starting hex and end the turn. Both fighters keep the damage already dealt. Pressing ROLL instead gives up the retreat option for this battle. The CPU can also retreat when attacking. Both units roll a D8. The higher die attacks and deals its class's HIT value; equal rolls do no damage. Exchanges continue until a troop falls. If the attacker wins, it moves into the defeated troop's hex. The survivor's winning bonus is another D8, giving 1–8 Health without exceeding its maximum. The button reveals the deterministic reward, so both players' matches agree. The losing side's bonus is revealed automatically when necessary. Red dice represent Red and blue dice represent Blue.

VICTORY AND FORTRESSES
Tap either Fortress to see its current Health, maximum Health and Defense Hit, even when it is out of attack range. Destroy the enemy Fortress to win. A Fortress has 50 Health, cannot move or attack proactively, and fires a fireball for five damage whenever its defensive D8 wins a battle exchange. If both armies are eliminated before either Fortress falls, both use trebuchets in Final Siege: a D8 of 4–8 deals 7 damage to the enemy Fortress, simultaneously. Both Fortresses may fall at once. If a side has no legal move or attack, it passes. Two consecutive forced passes end the match in a stalemate.

MODES
Single Player: Red versus Blue CPU. Pass & Play: two players share a device. Online 2-Player: Zompa manages invitations, turn syncing, and match delivery. Back, Help, Sound and Pause are host toolbar controls.

UPDATING FROM OLD BOARD VERSIONS
The new combat stats use a new local save format. Start a new Solo or Pass & Play match after updating. Existing online matches use their pinned version.

VISUAL INDICATORS
The illuminated unit is selected; gold outlines indicate possible moves, and red crossed swords indicate an attack destination. A faint outline marks troops crossed by a jump or teleport. Injured units show a small health bar. Battle damage rises near the HP bar, and the arcade victory banner precedes the ROLL / CONTINUE control.

ATTACK APPROACH
The target remains on its hex while the attacker moves onto it, above the defender. Only after the approach does the D8 battle decide the outcome.

maximum Health (for example HEALTH 17 / 22), MOVE and HIT. No player numbers appear in
this card. Soldier MOVE 1 reaches the adjacent forward hexes; Wizard MOVE 2 may
