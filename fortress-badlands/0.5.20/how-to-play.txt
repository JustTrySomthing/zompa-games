FORTRESS: THE BADLANDS — HOW TO PLAY

Win by destroying the enemy Fortress. Red starts at the bottom and Blue at the top. Each side has 15 troops and a Fortress. At the start, each side rolls a D12. The higher roll goes first. Ties reroll.

YOUR TURN
Select one piece and choose one action. Gold hexes show where it can move. Crossed swords mark enemies it can attack. Movement ends on an empty hex. Attacking leaves your piece on its current hex, even if it defeats the enemy.

Soldiers move one hex forward. Ogres move one hex in any direction. Barbarians move up to two hexes and can turn. Assassins move any distance along a clear straight line. Wizards teleport up to two hexes to an empty hex. Archers move up to two hexes on a clear straight line.

ATTACK RANGE
Most troops attack adjacent enemies. Archers shoot arrows at enemies within three hexes. Wizards cast fireballs at enemies within two hexes. Both can fire over other pieces. A Fortress is stationary. After an enemy attempts to attack it, it can launch boulders on its own turns at targets in the three hex rings in front of it.

BATTLES
Choose Attack Now, then Roll to stage the attack. A D12 roll must be higher than the target's Shield. A roll equal to or below Shield is repelled and ends the turn. A landed attack removes Health equal to the attacker's Power, down to zero. The defender deals no damage.

When you defeat a target while injured, choose Roll for Bonus. A piece at full Health skips the bonus roll. The D12 restores up to the rolled amount of Health, capped at the attacker's maximum. Press Continue after the result. The CPU rolls automatically.

FINAL SIEGE
If both armies are gone and both Fortresses survive, their remaining Health carries into the Final Siege. Both Fortresses roll a D12 at the same time. The higher roll deals 8 damage, and ties deal none. Volleys continue until one Fortress is destroyed.

STATS
Power and Shield each use a scale of 1–12. Tap a piece for current and maximum Health, Move, Power, and Shield.

Piece         Health  Power  Shield
Soldier           16      8       2
Assassin          18     10       3
Ogre              24     12       5
Barbarian         25     11       5
Fortress          30      8       3
Wizard            18     10       4
Archer            14      6       3

ZOMPA MODES
Play solo against the CPU, pass and play on one device, or play online. Start a new match to roll for the first turn. Existing online matches use their original version.
