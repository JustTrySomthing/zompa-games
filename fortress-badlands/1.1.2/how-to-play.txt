FORTRESS SIEGE: FINAL CONQUEST

Defend your Fortress. Destroy the enemy Fortress to win.

For hands-on practice, choose How to Play on the opening screen. The tutorial uses a separate board.

START A MATCH
Choose Start Game, then press Roll. Each side gets a twelve-sided die (D12). The higher roll goes first. Ties reroll automatically. In an online match, the player who sent the invite presses Roll.

TAKE YOUR TURN
Choose one move or one attack. Tap a friendly piece to see its options.

Move: Tap a gold hex, then Move Here. The destination must be empty.
Attack: Tap an enemy with an icy-blue rim. The brighter outline and Target card show whom you selected. Tap Attack Now. Your attacker stays on its hex.

READ A CARD
Tap a troop or Fortress from either side. The down arrow on its compact card opens its abilities and tactics. Tap the up arrow to return.

Health shows current / maximum. Move and Range count hexes. Power is the damage a standard hit deals. Shield is the number the attack roll must beat.

KNOW YOUR PIECES
Soldier — Moves one hex forward. Attacks any adjacent enemy, including one behind it.
Ogre — Moves one hex in any direction. Hits hard at close range.
Barbarian — Moves up to two hexes and can turn along the way. Attacks adjacent enemies.
Archer — Moves up to two hexes along a clear straight line. Shoots over troops up to three hexes in a straight line.
Assassin — Moves any distance along a clear straight line. Strikes up to two hexes in a straight line, even past another troop.
Wizard — Teleports up to two hexes to an empty hex, over intervening pieces. Casts up to two hexes in any direction, even over troops.

DEFEND YOUR FORTRESS
Each Fortress starts with 50 Health and holds its starting hex. Its expanded card shows Status: Dormant until the first enemy attack, even if that attack deals no damage. It then shows Status: Armed. On your turn, you can fire a boulder over troops up to three hexes into the rows ahead, or at an enemy on a hex directly beside the Fortress. Firing uses your turn.

BATTLES AND REWARDS
After Ready for Combat, the attack die rolls automatically. A roll above the defender’s Shield lands a hit for the attacker’s Power. An equal or lower roll is repelled. The defender takes no damage from a repelled attack.

If your injured fighter defeats an enemy in a standard battle, tap Roll for Reward. The die restores Health up to the fighter’s maximum. A full-health winner skips the reward roll. The CPU rolls its own rewards.

CRITICAL HEALTH AND DEATH MATCH
Pieces at one-quarter Health or lower pulse. If both combatants are critical, a skull marks the selected target. Confirming the attack starts a Death Match. Each side rolls automatically with an equal chance to win. Ties reroll. The loser is defeated instantly, regardless of Shield or Power. The winner keeps its current Health.

FINAL SIEGE AND STALEMATE
If both armies are gone and both Fortresses survive, the Fortresses duel with their remaining Health. Each rolls a D12 per volley. The higher roll deals 8 damage. A tie deals none. Volleys continue until a Fortress falls.

If a side has no legal action, it must pass. Two consecutive passes end the match in a draw.

Online play: The inviter starts the match and rolls for first turn. During battle, the attacker advances the result and rolls any health reward. Both players see the roll and the health gained.

ADVANCED SETTINGS
Open the Zompa toolbar gear and choose Advanced Settings before starting a new match. Both optional rules start off. The inviter sets the rules for both players online. Once play starts, the rules stay fixed for that match.

Booby Traps: Start with 1–9 hidden traps in the three middle rows (default 3). A troop triggers a trap only by ending a move on its hex. Each trap goes off once and rolls 1–12 damage. Wall sections cannot hide traps.

Border Wall: Each hex in the center row starts with its own wall section. Set each section’s Health (default 16) and Shield (default 3). Select a troop, select a reachable wall section, then confirm Attack Now. A D12 roll above the Shield deals the attacker’s Power in damage. Attacking uses your turn. A destroyed section opens its hex for movement and does not award Health. Wall sections block shots across the board unless Allow ranged fire over the wall is checked. That option lets ranged projectiles cross the wall. Teleportation still needs an open route.
